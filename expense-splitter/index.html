Flexible splits

Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentage, or by shares. Rounding is deterministic, so shares always sum to the total.

Multi-currency

Log expenses in any of 10 major currencies. Every expense stores its exchange rate, so historical balances stay stable.

Balances that add up

Net balances per member, plus pairwise settlement suggestions that minimize the number of payments to get everyone square.

Private by default

Single-user and frontend-only: groups, expenses, and settlements persist to localStorage. Nothing leaves your browser.
